Label the assignments that count

This chapter carries your course AI-use expectation into the assignments where it matters most.

You’ll leave with

Labels and short AI statements for the few assignments in one course where students show what they can really do.

In Chapter 6 you wrote a course expectation and labeled one assignment. This chapter takes the next step without turning it into a project.

Start with the assignments that count

You don’t need to label every quiz and discussion post. Your course expectation already covers the everyday work. Put your attention on the few assignments where you need to see what a student can do on their own. Those are usually:

  • the big case write-ups, care plans, or projects
  • assignments tied to the skills or competencies your program signs off on
  • the work you’d point to if someone asked how you know a student is ready for clinicals

Those are also the assignments where students worry most about getting it wrong. A clear label and a sentence about why take the guessing out of it.

Match the label to what you need to see

Ask what the assignment has to show. If it has to show that a student can reason through a case on their own, No AI may be right. If what matters is the final judgment and AI can help with brainstorming or wording, AI-supported may fit. If part of the skill is using AI well and checking it, AI-integrated could be the honest choice. It’s fine for different assignments in the same course to get different labels.
