Part 1 · Before we meet · Chapter 3

Let AI take one of your tests

About 15 minutes

You’ll leave with

Real evidence about one of your own tests or assignments: what happens when a student hands it to AI. This is what we’ll work with on December 1.

Your students have the same tools you just used. This chapter lets you see your own test the way a student with AI would see it. Whatever happens, it’s useful. If AI does well, you’ve learned something important about that test. If it struggles, you’ll want to know why.

Pick your test

Choose something you’ll give again:

  • A quiz or a section of an exam
  • A written assignment or discussion prompt
  • A case write-up or care plan

Something written works best. Skills check-offs aren’t a fit here, since AI can’t place a catheter or restrain a patient. That’s worth noticing, though, and we’ll talk about it.

Before you paste anything, take out student names and anything confidential. Leave the answer key out too.

No test of your own yet? Use the ready-made case assignment and rubric.

Please don’t fix it yet

If you’re like most clinicians, you’ll want to start fixing your test right away. Hold off until December 1. Leave it exactly as it is for now. What AI did with it is the evidence we’ll work from together, and you’ll decide on one good change during the session.
