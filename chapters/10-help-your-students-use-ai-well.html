Part 3 · Back in your course · Chapter 10

Help your students use AI well

About 15 minutes

Works toward · Create

Design an AI-informed teaching approach and a clear course AI-use expectation for your context.

This chapter turns your course AI-use expectation into a one-page handout your students can keep.

You’ll leave with

A one-page handout for your students: your course expectation plus a few plain habits for using AI in ways that help them learn.

Most of your students are already using AI, and most of them figured it out on their own. They know how to get an answer. Fewer know how to tell whether the answer is any good, or when using it gets in the way of learning the skill.

They’re building a clinical eye too

Your students are at the start of building the clinical eye you already have. AI can help or hurt that. If a student checks AI’s drug calculation against their own, they’re practicing the habit that keeps patients safe. If they copy it without checking, they skip the practice and pick up a risky habit.

A few plain habits make the difference, and students follow them more readily when you say them out loud and put them in writing. That’s what the handout below is for.

Keep it short

A handout students actually read beats a long policy they skim. Pick the habits that matter most in your course, and leave the rest for a conversation (Chapter 11 helps with that).
