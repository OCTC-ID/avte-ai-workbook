New here? How this workbook works

You already have a clinical eye. It’s what catches the wrong dose before anyone else does. This fall you’ve been building a teaching eye, the one that notices when a quiz doesn’t match what students are supposed to learn. Anything AI gives you deserves a look from both.

What you’ll be able to do

These are the two objectives for Week 7 from the academy syllabus. Each chapter works toward one of them.

Evaluate

Evaluate the benefits, risks, and ethics of generative AI in teaching and assessment.

You’ll work on this in Chapters 1 to 4.

Create

Design an AI-informed teaching approach and a clear course AI-use expectation for your context.

You’ll work on this in Chapters 5 and 6. Part 3 helps you carry it into your course.

How this workbook works

This workbook goes with Week 7 of the Association of Veterinary Technician Educators (AVTE) Vet Tech New Faculty Academy, and it’s yours to keep.

  • Your answers save in this browser as you type, so you can stop and come back.
  • Nothing you type here is sent anywhere. It stays on your device.
  • Each chapter ends with a button to print or save a PDF. That’s the way to keep a copy or move your work to another device.
  • It works on a phone. Chapter 3 is easier on a computer, since you’ll be copying and pasting.
  • Any free AI tool works for everything here: ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, Google Gemini, Claude, or another you like.
What to bring on December 1

The test or assignment you used in Chapter 3, AI’s answer, and the score you gave it. If you print or save Chapter 3 as a PDF, that covers all three.
