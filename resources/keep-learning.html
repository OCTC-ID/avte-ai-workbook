Appendix

Keep learning

The books and resources behind this workbook, for when you’re ready for your next change.

Small teaching

  • Lang, J. M. (2021). Small Teaching: Everyday Lessons from the Science of Learning (2nd ed.). Jossey-Bass.

    The book behind the “one meaningful change” idea in Chapter 6. Short chapters, each with small activities you can try in an existing course. The first edition came out in 2016.

  • Darby, F., with Lang, J. M. (2019). Small Teaching Online: Applying Learning Science in Online Classes. Jossey-Bass.

    The same approach applied to online and hybrid courses. Useful if part of your program runs in Canvas, Blackboard, or another LMS.

Clear assignments

  • TILT Higher Ed resources, Mary-Ann Winkelmes

    Sample assignments, checklists, and research on transparent assignment design. Chapter 4 introduces it.

AI literacy

  • AI for Education. (2026). The SEE Framework: A Practical Guide to Building Generative AI Literacy.

    Safe, ethical, and effective use of generative AI, with an activity bank for adult learners. Chapter 8 describes it, and Chapter 12 adapts three of its activities. Free.

  • Liang, W., Yuksekgonul, M., Mao, Y., Wu, E., & Zou, J. (2023). GPT detectors are biased against non-native English writers. Patterns, 4(7). doi.org/10.1016/j.patter.2023.100779

    The study behind the advice in Chapter 11 not to rely on AI detectors.

  • Dell’Acqua, F., et al. (2023). Navigating the Jagged Technological Frontier. Harvard Business School Working Paper 24-013.

    Where the term “jagged frontier” comes from, used in Chapter 12’s “Where does AI do well?” activity.
