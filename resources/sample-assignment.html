Ready-made case assignment

Copy everything in the box below into your AI tool, after the prompt from Chapter 3. Then score AI’s answer with the rubric underneath.

Case: Daisy, dental cleaning

Daisy is a 10-year-old spayed female Labrador retriever scheduled for a dental cleaning under general anesthesia. At check-in, her owner mentions that Daisy has been drinking more water than usual for the past few weeks.

Answer all three parts. Write in complete sentences.

  1. Before anesthesia. What would you want to check or ask about before Daisy is induced? Explain why each item matters, and explain how the owner’s comment changes your plan.
  2. During anesthesia. Which parameters would you monitor? For each one, describe a change that would make you alert the veterinarian right away.
  3. At discharge. Write what you would tell Daisy’s owner at pickup, in plain language a client would understand.

Rubric

Score each row, then add them up. 9 points possible.

What you’re looking forReady (3)Developing (2)Not yet (1)
Clinical reasoning before anesthesia Names appropriate checks and explains why each matters. Connects the increased thirst to a specific change in the plan. Names appropriate checks, but the reasons are thin or the owner’s comment isn’t connected to the plan. Lists checks without reasons, or misses the owner’s comment.
Monitoring during anesthesia Names the key parameters and a clear, specific change in each that would prompt alerting the veterinarian. Names most parameters; the changes to watch for are vague. Few parameters, or no sense of when to alert the veterinarian.
Client communication at discharge Plain, warm, accurate, and specific about what to watch for at home and when to call. Accurate, but uses clinical terms a client may not understand, or is missing what to watch for. Unclear, inaccurate, or not written for a client.
A note for your teaching eye

As you score, ask yourself: does this answer show me what a student can do, or what a student can find? Hold onto that thought for December 1.
