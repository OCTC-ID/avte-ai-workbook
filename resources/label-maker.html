Assignment label maker

Make a small image that shows students where AI fits on an assignment.

Pick a label, add a short line if you want one, and save it as a PNG. Then put it at the top of the assignment in your course, next to the written instructions.

  • No AI. Students do this work without AI because the thinking is the point.
  • AI-supported. Students can use AI along the way, for example to brainstorm or to quiz themselves. The work they turn in is their own.
  • AI-integrated. Using AI is part of the task, and students show how they used it.

Chapter 7 walks you through choosing a label and writing the statement that goes with it.

Use it with words, too

The label is a quick signal. Students still need the sentence or two that says what’s allowed and why, written in the assignment instructions. Chapter 7 helps you write it.
